🇬🇧 English

I am a retired Civil and Hydraulic Engineering Technician, with decades of experience in hydraulic engineering, slope stability, and terrain modeling.

My current research focuses on mathematical visualization, implicit geometry, and GPU-based point cloud reconstruction.

This repository introduces: MCA_WLTNT (Marching Cube Algorithm Without Lookup Table and No Triangle), a geometry-first approach to iso-surface reconstruction.

Key idea:
Implicit Surface → Edge Sampling → Iso-points → Point Cloud Surface

The method removes both lookup tables and triangle mesh construction, making it highly suitable for GPU parallel computing, real-time simulation, and AI geometry systems.
